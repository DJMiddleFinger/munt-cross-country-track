What MUNT means

Four words, one team

Our name is our standard. Every runner, jumper and thrower who pulls on a MUNT singlet signs on to all four.

  • Morally

    We do what is right in competition and in the hallway. Honest effort, honest results.

  • United

    Distance runners, sprinters and field athletes train on one team with one goal.

  • Nobly

    We win with grace, lose with respect, and lift up the athlete next to us.

  • Together

    Nobody runs the last lap alone. We start as a team and we finish as a team.
